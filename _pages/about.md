I am currently a Member of Technical Staff at [Etched](https://www.etched.com/), working on model architecture and 
inference. I recently graduated from Stanford University with a BS in Mathematics and a BSH in Computer Science (systems), minoring in Economics. While at Stanford, I worked on mechanism design with [Prof. Ravi Jagadeesan](https://sites.google.com/site/ravijagadeesan/), direct preference optimization and causal inference with [Prof. Vasilis Syrgkanis](https://vsyrgkanis.com/), and mathematical genetics with [Prof. Noah Rosenberg](https://rosenberglab.stanford.edu/index.html). During college, I interned at [Citadel Securities](https://www.citadelsecurities.com) as a quantitative researcher and [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/) as a software engineer. Before that, I did research with the [ASSISTments Lab](https://new.assistments.org/) and [MIT PRIMES](https://math.mit.edu/research/highschool/primes/). I graduated from the [Massachusetts Academy of Math & Science at WPI](https://www.massacademy.org/) in 2022.

I am also a classical pianist and cellist. Please email me at kvseet17 'at' stanford 'dot' edu if you'd like to chat or collaborate!
